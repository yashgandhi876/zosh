HRA exemption calculator (2026-27, new 50% cities)

HRA exemption calculator for tax year 2026-27. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad now get 50% of salary, like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai.

Open the calculator

The HRA exemption is the least of: the HRA you receive; rent paid minus 10% of basic + DA; and 50% of basic + DA in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune or Ahmedabad (40% elsewhere). The four new cities apply from tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026. HRA is exempt only in the old regime.
